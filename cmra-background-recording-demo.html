Camera 1.0.8: background video recording

Google Play reviewer demonstration of the signed release on an Android 16 emulator. The virtual camera scene is test content. No real cloud destination receives this recording.

  1. Camera opens visibly. Select video and press Record; camera and microphone privacy indicators appear.
  2. Press Home. The user-started recording continues through the foreground service.
  3. Open notifications. The silent recording notification shows elapsed time and Pause/Resume and Stop controls. Pause and resume update the recording state.
  4. Stop ends recording and removes the recording notification. Return to Camera.

Recording starts only from Camera while visible. It does not restart automatically after reboot or process termination. Android privacy and battery restrictions can interrupt it. Microphone access is used only when the user allows audio.
