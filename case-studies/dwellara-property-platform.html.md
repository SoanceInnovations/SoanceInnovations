# Creating a High-Trust Property Discovery Experience for Dwellara

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/dwellara-property-platform.html

## Summary / TLDR

Dwellara is a residential property advisory helping families evaluate new developments. The team needed a credible digital presence that made projects, amenities, floor plans, and enquiries easy to navigate.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

Dwellara is a fictional real estate company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a credible digital presence that made projects, amenities, floor plans, and enquiries easy to navigate while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Digital strategy and content architecture

- Responsive website design

- CMS implementation and migration

- Accessibility, technical SEO, performance, and security

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Project comparison and location discovery: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Interactive floor plans and availability: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Lead routing to the correct property advisor: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We clarified the story before designing a modular page system, then delivered a responsive CMS build with structured content, strong performance budgets, and measurable conversion paths.

- Designed and validated project comparison and location discovery as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated interactive floor plans and availability as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated lead routing to the correct property advisor as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.
