# Rebuilding PixelCrest’s Website for Speed, Security, and Qualified Leads

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/pixelcrest-agency-website.html

## Summary / TLDR

PixelCrest is a strategy and digital experience studio serving growth-stage companies. The team needed a modern website that loaded quickly, communicated expertise clearly, and converted more qualified visitors.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

PixelCrest is a fictional digital services company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a modern website that loaded quickly, communicated expertise clearly, and converted more qualified visitors while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Digital strategy and content architecture

- Responsive website design

- CMS implementation and migration

- Accessibility, technical SEO, performance, and security

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Modular service and case-study pages: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Core Web Vitals and technical SEO improvements: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Secure forms and a flexible editorial workflow: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We clarified the story before designing a modular page system, then delivered a responsive CMS build with structured content, strong performance budgets, and measurable conversion paths.

- Designed and validated modular service and case-study pages as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated Core Web Vitals and technical SEO improvements as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated secure forms and a flexible editorial workflow as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

Because PixelCrest is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.
