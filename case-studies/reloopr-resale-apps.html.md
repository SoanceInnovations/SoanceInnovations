# Connecting Collectors and Retailers through ReLoopr’s Resale Apps

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/reloopr-resale-apps.html

## Summary / TLDR

ReLoopr is a circular-commerce network helping businesses trade reusable materials and pre-owned goods. The team needed separate mobile experiences for collectors and retailers backed by one reliable transaction platform.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

ReLoopr is a fictional circular commerce company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver separate mobile experiences for collectors and retailers backed by one reliable transaction platform while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Product strategy and user research

- Mobile UX and interface design

- Cross-platform application development

- API integration, QA, and launch support

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Role-specific Android and iOS workflows: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Pickup requests and offer negotiation: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Inventory, settlement, and transaction history: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We designed the experience around short, confidence-building tasks, then built a cross-platform application backed by secure APIs, analytics, and a maintainable design system.

- Designed and validated role-specific Android and iOS workflows as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated pickup requests and offer negotiation as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated inventory, settlement, and transaction history as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.
