# Improving Orthopedic Product Access through the Stepvera Mobile Store

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/stepvera-orthopedic-commerce.html

## Summary / TLDR

Stepvera is a consumer health brand offering supports, braces, and mobility products. The team needed a guided mobile shopping experience that matched users with appropriate products and care information.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

Stepvera is a fictional consumer healthcare company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a guided mobile shopping experience that matched users with appropriate products and care information while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Product strategy and user research

- Mobile UX and interface design

- Cross-platform application development

- API integration, QA, and launch support

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Symptom- and activity-led product discovery: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Fit guidance and educational content: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Saved routines, reorders, and support: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We designed the experience around short, confidence-building tasks, then built a cross-platform application backed by secure APIs, analytics, and a maintainable design system.

- Designed and validated symptom- and activity-led product discovery as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated fit guidance and educational content as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated saved routines, reorders, and support as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

## Illustrative outcomes

Because Stepvera is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.
