# Transforming the Digital Campus Experience for NovaArc Institute

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/novaarc-institute-portal.html

## Summary / TLDR

NovaArc Institute is a multidisciplinary engineering institute serving students, faculty, researchers, and industry partners. The team needed a unified institutional platform for programs, research, admissions, notices, and decentralized publishing.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

NovaArc Institute is a fictional higher education company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a unified institutional platform for programs, research, admissions, notices, and decentralized publishing while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Platform strategy and requirements definition

- User experience and interface design

- Full-stack application development

- Cloud architecture, security, analytics, and support

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Multi-department content governance: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Student, research, and admissions journeys: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- High-availability publishing and search: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We translated the operating model into a role-based platform, validated the highest-risk workflows early, and built a scalable web application with secure integrations and observable infrastructure.

- Designed and validated multi-department content governance as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated student, research, and admissions journeys as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated high-availability publishing and search as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

## Illustrative outcomes

Because NovaArc Institute is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.
