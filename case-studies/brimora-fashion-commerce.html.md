# Launching a Bold Direct-to-Consumer Storefront for Brimora

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/brimora-fashion-commerce.html

## Summary / TLDR

Brimora is a contemporary accessories label focused on versatile caps and everyday essentials. The team needed a mobile-first storefront that balanced editorial brand expression with frictionless shopping.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

Brimora is a fictional fashion retail company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a mobile-first storefront that balanced editorial brand expression with frictionless shopping while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Commerce strategy and information architecture

- Responsive UX and visual design

- Storefront and platform development

- Search, checkout, analytics, and performance optimization

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Collection-led product discovery: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Size, color, and stock variants: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Fast checkout and lifecycle marketing integrations: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We mapped discovery and purchase journeys, created a modular commerce design system, and implemented a fast storefront connected to catalog, checkout, content, and analytics services.

- Designed and validated collection-led product discovery as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated size, color, and stock variants as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated fast checkout and lifecycle marketing integrations as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

## Illustrative outcomes

Because Brimora is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.

- Faster product discovery and a more confident path to purchase

- Simpler merchandising and content operations

- A flexible commerce foundation ready for new categories and campaigns
