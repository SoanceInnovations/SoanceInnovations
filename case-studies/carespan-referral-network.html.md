# Streamlining Specialist Referrals and Care Collaboration for CareSpan

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/carespan-referral-network.html

## Summary / TLDR

CareSpan is a care-coordination network linking primary practitioners with specialist teams. The team needed a clearer mobile workflow for referrals, case updates, secure messaging, and follow-up responsibilities.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

CareSpan is a fictional healthcare collaboration company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a clearer mobile workflow for referrals, case updates, secure messaging, and follow-up responsibilities while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Product strategy and user research

- Mobile UX and interface design

- Cross-platform application development

- API integration, QA, and launch support

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Structured referral creation and triage: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Secure case-based communication: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Status tracking, reminders, and audit history: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We designed the experience around short, confidence-building tasks, then built a cross-platform application backed by secure APIs, analytics, and a maintainable design system.

- Designed and validated structured referral creation and triage as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated secure case-based communication as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated status tracking, reminders, and audit history as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

## Illustrative outcomes

Because CareSpan is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.
