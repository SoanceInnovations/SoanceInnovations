# Making Personal Finance Easier to Understand with RupeeRoot

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/rupeeroot-financial-learning.html

## Summary / TLDR

RupeeRoot is an independent financial education platform for first-time investors and young families. The team needed a trusted content experience that made complex money decisions practical, searchable, and approachable.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

RupeeRoot is a fictional financial education company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a trusted content experience that made complex money decisions practical, searchable, and approachable while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Content strategy and information architecture

- Editorial experience design

- CMS and front-end development

- Search, structured data, analytics, and performance

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Structured learning paths and calculators: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Topic hubs with expert-reviewed articles: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Newsletter capture and personalized reading lists: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We organized the content around audience intent, created reusable editorial formats, and delivered a fast publishing platform with strong search, structured metadata, and accessible reading patterns.

- Designed and validated structured learning paths and calculators as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated topic hubs with expert-reviewed articles as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated newsletter capture and personalized reading lists as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

## Illustrative outcomes

Because RupeeRoot is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.
