# Supporting Independent Ageing with the EldaSense Health Companion

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/eldasense-senior-health.html

## Summary / TLDR

EldaSense is a digital wellness venture helping older adults and caregivers follow everyday health routines. The team needed an accessible mobile companion that combined check-ins, reminders, guided assessments, and caregiver insights.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

EldaSense is a fictional digital health company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver an accessible mobile companion that combined check-ins, reminders, guided assessments, and caregiver insights while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Product strategy and user research

- Mobile UX and interface design

- Cross-platform application development

- API integration, QA, and launch support

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Large-type accessible interaction patterns: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- AI-assisted wellness check-ins: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Caregiver permissions and escalation alerts: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We designed the experience around short, confidence-building tasks, then built a cross-platform application backed by secure APIs, analytics, and a maintainable design system.

- Designed and validated large-type accessible interaction patterns as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated AI-assisted wellness check-ins as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated caregiver permissions and escalation alerts as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.
