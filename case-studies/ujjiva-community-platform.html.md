# Extending Community Impact through Ujjiva Collective’s New Website

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/ujjiva-community-platform.html

## Summary / TLDR

Ujjiva Collective is a nonprofit supporting women, migrant families, and community-led livelihoods. The team needed a warm, accessible website for programs, impact reporting, volunteering, partnerships, and donations.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

Ujjiva Collective is a fictional social impact company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a warm, accessible website for programs, impact reporting, volunteering, partnerships, and donations while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Digital strategy and content architecture

- Responsive website design

- CMS implementation and migration

- Accessibility, technical SEO, performance, and security

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Human-centered program storytelling: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Impact dashboards and downloadable reports: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Donation, volunteer, and partner journeys: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We clarified the story before designing a modular page system, then delivered a responsive CMS build with structured content, strong performance budgets, and measurable conversion paths.

- Designed and validated human-centered program storytelling as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated impact dashboards and downloadable reports as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated donation, volunteer, and partner journeys as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.
