# Clarifying a Complex Advisory Offer through Ambiqo’s Website

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/ambiqo-advisory-website.html

## Summary / TLDR

Ambiqo is a consultancy helping leadership teams navigate ambiguous strategic and operational decisions. The team needed a focused website that translated a nuanced offer into clear services, proof, and conversion paths.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

Ambiqo is a fictional business advisory company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a focused website that translated a nuanced offer into clear services, proof, and conversion paths while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Digital strategy and content architecture

- Responsive website design

- CMS implementation and migration

- Accessibility, technical SEO, performance, and security

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Audience-led service architecture: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We clarified the story before designing a modular page system, then delivered a responsive CMS build with structured content, strong performance budgets, and measurable conversion paths.

- Designed and validated audience-led service architecture as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated insight publishing and lead capture as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated modular case examples and consultant profiles as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.
