# Designing Connected Engineering Workflows for MotiveAxis

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/motiveaxis-engineering-apps.html

## Summary / TLDR

MotiveAxis is an engineering technology company supporting mobility and industrial product teams. The team needed two connected applications for field collaboration, issue capture, approvals, and program visibility.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

MotiveAxis is a fictional engineering technology company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver two connected applications for field collaboration, issue capture, approvals, and program visibility while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Workflow discovery and service design

- Enterprise UX and design systems

- Application engineering and integration

- Quality assurance, documentation, and release support

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Shared design system across both applications: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Offline field capture and synchronized records: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Role-based reviews, approvals, and analytics: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We mapped roles, decisions, and exception paths, designed a shared component system, and delivered modular workflows that integrate cleanly with the wider technology environment.

- Designed and validated shared design system across both applications as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated offline field capture and synchronized records as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated role-based reviews, approvals, and analytics as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.
