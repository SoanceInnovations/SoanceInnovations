# Creating an Urban Innovation Knowledge Hub for CivicNexa

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/civicnexa-knowledge-hub.html

## Summary / TLDR

CivicNexa is a city innovation initiative sharing practical programs, research, and technology pilots. The team needed an editorial platform that made complex civic programs understandable to residents and professional audiences.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

CivicNexa is a fictional urban innovation company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver an editorial platform that made complex civic programs understandable to residents and professional audiences while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Content strategy and information architecture

- Editorial experience design

- CMS and front-end development

- Search, structured data, analytics, and performance

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Program stories and searchable resources: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Multilingual editorial workflows: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Accessible data highlights and newsletters: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We organized the content around audience intent, created reusable editorial formats, and delivered a fast publishing platform with strong search, structured metadata, and accessible reading patterns.

- Designed and validated program stories and searchable resources as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated multilingual editorial workflows as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated accessible data highlights and newsletters as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

## Illustrative outcomes

Because CivicNexa is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.

- More discoverable content and stronger topic authority

- A faster, more consistent editorial workflow

- Improved reading experience across mobile and desktop
