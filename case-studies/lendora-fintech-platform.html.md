# Making Responsible Credit More Accessible with Lendora

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/lendora-fintech-platform.html

## Summary / TLDR

Lendora is a digital lending venture focused on transparent, user-friendly personal finance. The team needed a secure application journey that simplified eligibility, document collection, verification, and servicing.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

Lendora is a fictional financial technology company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a secure application journey that simplified eligibility, document collection, verification, and servicing while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Platform strategy and requirements definition

- User experience and interface design

- Full-stack application development

- Cloud architecture, security, analytics, and support

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Guided eligibility and application flows: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Document capture and verification integrations: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Application status, repayment, and support: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We translated the operating model into a role-based platform, validated the highest-risk workflows early, and built a scalable web application with secure integrations and observable infrastructure.

- Designed and validated guided eligibility and application flows as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated document capture and verification integrations as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated application status, repayment, and support as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.
