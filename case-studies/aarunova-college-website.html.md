# Creating an Accessible Digital Campus for Aarunova College

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/aarunova-college-website.html

## Summary / TLDR

Aarunova College is a health sciences college offering degree programs, clinical training, and community education. The team needed a modern institutional website for admissions, departments, notices, accreditation, and student resources.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

Aarunova College is a fictional higher education company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a modern institutional website for admissions, departments, notices, accreditation, and student resources while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Digital strategy and content architecture

- Responsive website design

- CMS implementation and migration

- Accessibility, technical SEO, performance, and security

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Program and department information architecture: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Admissions journeys and document downloads: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Notices, events, and governance publishing: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We clarified the story before designing a modular page system, then delivered a responsive CMS build with structured content, strong performance budgets, and measurable conversion paths.

- Designed and validated program and department information architecture as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated admissions journeys and document downloads as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated notices, events, and governance publishing as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.
