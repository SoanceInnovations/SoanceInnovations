# Serving a Faster, More Discoverable Recipe Experience for MasalaMuse

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/masalamuse-recipe-platform.html

## Summary / TLDR

MasalaMuse is a recipe publisher celebrating practical regional cooking for a global audience. The team needed a content platform that supported rich recipes, fast search, video, and dependable publishing at scale.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

MasalaMuse is a fictional food media company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a content platform that supported rich recipes, fast search, video, and dependable publishing at scale while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Content strategy and information architecture

- Editorial experience design

- CMS and front-end development

- Search, structured data, analytics, and performance

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Ingredient and cuisine filters: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Structured recipe data and printable views: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Editorial workflows for articles and video: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We organized the content around audience intent, created reusable editorial formats, and delivered a fast publishing platform with strong search, structured metadata, and accessible reading patterns.

- Designed and validated ingredient and cuisine filters as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated structured recipe data and printable views as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated editorial workflows for articles and video as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

## Illustrative outcomes

Because MasalaMuse is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.

- More discoverable content and stronger topic authority

- A faster, more consistent editorial workflow
