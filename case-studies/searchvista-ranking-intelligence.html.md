# Turning Search Data into Decisions with the SearchVista Intelligence Platform

Source: https://www.soance.com/case-studies/searchvista-ranking-intelligence.html

## Summary / TLDR

SearchVista is an SEO consultancy managing complex portfolios of search campaigns. The team needed a consolidated ranking and performance platform that transformed fragmented data into clear priorities.

Soance shaped the strategy, experience, and technical foundation around measurable user and operational needs, then translated the concept into a launch-ready digital product.

## Overview

SearchVista is a fictional marketing analytics company created for this representative portfolio story. Its operating context, user needs, and delivery constraints are modeled on realistic digital transformation engagements.

The core brief was to deliver a consolidated ranking and performance platform that transformed fragmented data into clear priorities while giving internal teams a system they could confidently operate, measure, and evolve after launch.

## Services provided

- Analytics discovery and data modeling

- Dashboard UX and information design

- Data integration and reporting development

- Permissions, alerts, and quality assurance

## Challenges

The project needed to balance a polished customer experience with the less-visible realities of content, operations, permissions, integrations, and long-term maintainability.

- Keyword and competitor monitoring: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Power BI reporting and scheduled data refreshes: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

- Client workspaces with actionable alerts: The experience needed to support this capability without adding unnecessary complexity for users or operational teams.

## Solution

We aligned the reporting model to real decisions, consolidated data sources, and designed progressive dashboards that move from portfolio-level signals to detailed actions.

- Designed and validated keyword and competitor monitoring as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated Power BI reporting and scheduled data refreshes as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

- Designed and validated client workspaces with actionable alerts as part of one coherent, role-aware product journey.

A shared design system, instrumentation plan, and staged release approach kept the product coherent while allowing the team to learn from realistic usage patterns.

## Illustrative outcomes

Because SearchVista is fictional, these outcomes describe the intended value of the solution rather than claims about a real client engagement.
