SOANCE INSIGHTS

Start with the work. Not the hype.

A practical starting point for your next automation project.

Look for the repetition.

Choose one workflow your team repeats: copying an order, checking a document, or updating a customer. Describe the steps before choosing the technology.

Make success measurable.

Decide what better looks like. It might be fewer manual steps, clearer handovers, or less time spent searching for information.

Keep people in control.

Build in a review step for important decisions. Start small, test with real users, and expand once the process works.
